# User guide

Screen names are given in English with the Bulgarian label in brackets.

## 1. First start – set up the plan (about 5 minutes)

1. Open **Budget (Бюджет)** in the bottom bar.
2. In the **Income (Приходи)** card tap ✏️ and enter:
   - **Monthly salary** – the amount every "% of salary" is calculated from.
   - **Food vouchers / month** – optional; they can be subtracted from the groceries plan.
   - **Share of extra income to investments** – e.g. 50%. The rest of the extra income goes to savings.
   - **Extra incomes** – scholarship, side job, family support… (tap *Add income* for each).
3. Tap **Add category (Добави категория)** for every budget line:
   - **Name** – e.g. *Rent*, *Groceries*, *Fuel*, *Subscriptions*, *ETF*.
   - **Type** – *Needs*, *Wants*, *Savings* or *Investments*.
   - **How it is planned** –
     - *Fixed amount*: a known bill (rent 40 €, internet 16 €…).
     - *% of salary*: a target share (groceries 12%, eating out 4%…). The app shows the € value immediately.
     - For groceries you can tick *Subtract food vouchers from the plan*.
4. The **Salary allocation** card shows how much of the salary is planned and what is still unallocated.
   Aim for *Fully allocated* – give every euro a job.

## 2. Every day – record what you spend

1. Tap **+ Add expense (Добави разход)** on *Overview* or *Expenses*.
2. Type the amount. For a receipt with several items you can type `4,40+7,10+3,40` – the total is shown.
3. Tap the category, change the date if it was not today, add a note if you like.
4. **Save.**

Shortcut: on *Overview* tap ⊕ next to a category – it is already selected for you.

For *Savings* and *Investments* categories, "add expense" means "I put this money aside".

## 3. Change or delete an expense

1. Open **Expenses (Разходи)** (or tap a category on *Overview* – the list opens filtered to it).
2. Tap the expense.
3. Change anything and **Save**, or tap **Delete**.

Use the arrows next to the month name to look at other months; tap the month name to jump back to today.

## 4. Reading the Overview

- **Spent this month** – needs + wants you recorded, against what you planned for them.
- **Saved + invested** – what you recorded in savings/investment categories, against the plan.
- **Unallocated** – salary that is not assigned to any category (red if the plan is bigger than the salary).
- Every category shows a progress bar and a status:
  - *Within plan* / *Over budget* for spending,
  - *Goal reached* / *Below goal* for savings and investments,
  - *No data yet* when nothing was recorded this month.

## 5. Goals

- **Emergency fund** – tap ✏️ to set how many months of *Needs* it should cover, what you already have, and
  how much you add per month. The app shows the target, progress and months left.
- **Savings goals** – *Add goal* for a laptop, holiday, Erasmus+… with target, saved so far and monthly amount.
  Tap a goal to update how much you have saved.
- **Investing** – shows how the monthly investment (your *Investments* categories + the investment share of
  extra incomes) could grow over 5–40 years at the return you set. It is an illustration, not a promise.

## 6. Backup – do this regularly

*Settings (⚙️) → Save backup* creates a small `.json` file. Save it to Google Drive or send it to yourself.
*Restore backup* replaces the current data with the file's data (for example on a new phone).
