# User guide

Screen names are given in English with the Bulgarian label in brackets.

## 1. First start – set up the plan (about 5 minutes)

1. Open **Budget (Бюджет)** in the bottom bar.
2. In the **Income (Приходи)** card tap ✏️ and enter:
   - **Monthly salary** – the amount every "% of salary" is calculated from.
   - **Food vouchers / month** – optional; they can be subtracted from the groceries plan.
   - **Share of extra income to investments** – e.g. 50%. The rest of the extra income goes to savings.
   - **Extra incomes** – scholarship, side job, family support… (tap *Add income* for each).
3. The common categories are already there (35 of them, grouped like *🏠 Housing & bills*,
   *🍽️ Food*, *🚗 Transport* …) with no amounts. **Tap a category to set its amount.** Categories
   showing "—" are not planned yet – leave them like that if you don't need them, or delete them.
   Use **Add category (Добави категория)** for anything missing. In the category form you can set:
   - **Name**.
   - **How it is planned** –
     - *Fixed amount*: a known bill (rent 40 €, internet 16 €…).
     - *% of salary*: a target share (groceries 12%, eating out 4%…). The app shows the € value immediately.
     - For groceries you can tick *Subtract food vouchers from the plan*.
   - **Icon (emoji)** – e.g. 🏠 or 🛒. One emoji; typing another replaces it.
   - **Group** – where it appears in the budget and the charts.
   - **Type** – *Needs*, *Wants*, *Savings* or *Investments*.
4. The **Salary allocation** card shows how much of the salary is planned and what is still unallocated.
   Aim for *Fully allocated* – give every euro a job.

## 2. Every day – record what you spend

1. Tap **+ Add expense (Добави разход)** on *Overview* or *Expenses*.
2. Type the amount. For a receipt with several items tap the **+** in the amount field between the
   prices (`4,40+7,10+3,40`) – the total is shown.
3. Tap the category. The ones you used last are shown as buttons; **All categories** opens the
   full list.
4. The date is **Today** unless you tap **Yesterday** or **Pick a date**. Add a note if you like.
5. **Save** – the button stays above the keyboard, so there is nothing to scroll or close first.

Shortcuts:
- On *Overview* tap ⊕ next to a category – it is already selected for you.
- Press and hold the app's icon on the home screen → **Add expense** opens the form directly.
- Under the note field the app offers the notes you used before for that category (`Lidl`, `кафе` …) – one tap.

### Never miss an expense – daily reminder

*Settings (⚙️) → Reminder → Remind me every day.* Pick the time (20:30 by default). At that time
the phone shows *"Did you record today's expenses?"* with what is recorded so far; **tap the
notification and the new-expense form opens**. Tick *Only if nothing is recorded yet today* if you
want it to stay quiet on days you already logged something. The first time, Android asks whether the
app may show notifications – choose *Allow* (if you declined, the card shows an *Allow notifications*
button). The reminder keeps working after the phone restarts.

### Payments that repeat – rent, subscriptions, instalments

When you record such a payment, tick **Repeat every month** at the bottom of the form. From the
next month on, the app adds it by itself on the same day (the 31st becomes the last day in shorter
months) and tells you when you open it. All regular payments are listed in **Budget → Regular
payments**; tap ✕ to stop one – expenses already recorded stay. To change the amount, stop it and
record the payment again with the new amount.

For *Savings* and *Investments* categories, "add expense" means "I put this money aside".

## 3. Change or delete an expense

1. Open **Expenses (Разходи)** (or tap a category on *Overview* – the list opens filtered to it).
2. Tap the expense.
3. Change anything and **Save**, or tap the 🗑 bin to delete it. Deleted by mistake? Tap **Undo**
   in the message at the bottom of the screen.

Use the arrows next to the month name to look at other months; tap the month name to jump back to today.

**Find an expense:** type in the search box on *Expenses* – part of a note (`lidl`), a category
name or an amount (`38,5`). Search looks through all months, not only the one on screen.

The card on top shows what you **spent** in the month and, separately, what you **saved + invested**,
so "spent" is the same number as on *Overview*.

## 4. Reading the Overview

- **Spent this month** – needs + wants you recorded, against what you planned for them. During the
  current month it also shows **≈ how much you can spend per day** for the remaining days to end on plan.
- **Saved + invested** – what you recorded in savings/investment categories, against the plan.
- **Unallocated** – salary that is not assigned to any category (red if the plan is bigger than the salary).
- **This week** – spending from Monday to today against the weekly guide; tap it for the weekly report.
- **Spending through the month** – a line of your running total (blue) next to last month (grey) and
  the plan (dashed). Above the plan line means you are spending faster than planned.
- Categories with a plan or with spending are listed by group. Every category shows a progress bar and a status:
  - *Within plan* / *Over budget* for spending,
  - *Goal reached* / *Below goal* for savings and investments,
  - *No data yet* when nothing was recorded this month.

## 5. Reports (Отчети)

Open **Reports** in the bottom bar, or tap the *This week* card on *Overview*.

- **Week (Седмица)** – Monday to Sunday. Use the arrows to move between weeks; tap the dates to
  jump back to the current week. You see:
  - what you spent this week against the **weekly guide** (your monthly plan for needs + wants
    divided into weeks) and whether you spent more or less than the week before,
  - the average per day and what you saved this week,
  - a bar per day with a dashed "guide/day" line – **tap a bar** to list that day's expenses,
  - spending by category and all expenses of the week (tap one to edit it).
- **Month (Месец)** – a donut of spending by group (tap a slice or a row), cash flow (how the income
  splits into spent, saved and left), spending through the month compared with last month and the
  plan, and the biggest categories.
- **Trends (Тенденции)** – spending and savings for the last 6 months with the plan line, the
  monthly average and the average per category. Averages use finished months only – the month
  still in progress would make them look lower than they are.

On the line chart you can tap or slide your finger to read the value for any day.

## 6. Goals

- **Emergency fund** – tap ✏️ to set how many months of *Needs* it should cover, what you already have, and
  how much you add per month. The app shows the target, progress and months left.
- **Savings goals** – *Add goal* for a laptop, holiday, Erasmus+… with target, saved so far and monthly amount.
  Tap a goal to update how much you have saved.
- **Investing** – shows how the monthly investment (your *Investments* categories + the investment share of
  extra incomes) could grow over 5–40 years at the return you set. It is an illustration, not a promise.

## 7. Backup – do this regularly

*Settings (⚙️) → Save backup* creates a small `.json` file. Save it to Google Drive or send it to yourself.
*Restore backup* replaces the current data with the file's data (for example on a new phone).
A file that is not a Finance Plan backup is refused and your data stays as it is.

*Export expenses (CSV)* saves a table of every expense that opens in Excel or Google Sheets.
It is for your own analysis – it is **not** a backup and cannot be restored into the app.
